    About this tool

    • UCL is the share of a plant’s possible output lost to unplanned events. Low is under 2%, Medium is 2 to 6%, High is over 6%.
    • Design only needs no operating record. With past performance also reads two average UCL values from the IAEA PRIS database, for 2010 to 2013 and 2014 to 2017.
    • Age counts to 2024. Construction time runs from the start of construction to first grid connection.
    • The squares show the model’s score, one per point, filled clockwise from the top. The small bars show what pushed the result, compared with a typical reactor. The lighter band on a slider is the range the model learned from.
    • Medium is the hardest class.
    • Research tool built on IAEA PRIS and RDS-2 data. Not for operating, safety or licensing decisions.
