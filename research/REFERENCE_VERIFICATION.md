## C. Corrections to the V5 text found by comparing sentences with sources

1. Prantikos et al. were described as forecasting outages of "United States" reactors. The source says only "commercial light water reactors". Fixed.
2. Ejigu et al. were said to cover "autonomous control". The abstract does not say that. Fixed.
3. Zio (2022) was called a review. It is a perspective. Fixed.
4. Zubair and Akram (2023) could not be verified (the co-author could not be confirmed), so it was removed. Eleven more references from V5 were removed because the shorter literature review no longer needs them or because they were only partly checked: Ma and Jiang 2011, Ma et al. 2025, Gurgen and Dinh 2022, Nguyen and Diab 2023, Li and Lin 2021, Rivas et al. 2024a and 2024b, Che et al. 2022, Azhar et al. 2022, Dietterich 2000 and Zubair and Akram 2024. The sentences that cited them were removed from the text. Thirteen further references were checked and added (see section A), so the V6 list has 48 entries.
5. The introduction statistic (2023 figures from the 2024 report) was replaced by the 2024 figures of the 2025 report.
6. V5 said "the war in Ukraine (15 Ukrainian reactors are in the data)" and that its influence was not studied. In fact PRIS has no UCL values for the Ukrainian reactors in 2022 to 2024 (RDS-2 2025 says those data had not been submitted), so their labels rest on 2018 to 2021 only. The paper now says this.
